Frequently asked

Do you have a minimum order for business uniforms? +

No minimum. We'll embroider a single polo if that's all you need. Volume discounts start at 12 pieces (8% off) and go to 25% off at 100+ pieces.

How long does a business order take? +

Standard turnaround is 5-10 business days from proof approval. Rush orders (2-3 days) are available at +25% for tight deadlines. Order 3-4 weeks before an event for a stress-free timeline.

Can I set up an ongoing uniform program? +

Yes. Once we've digitized your logo and confirmed the pieces you use (polos, hats, work shirts, jackets), reorders are a single text to Kristen. We keep your specs on file so every batch matches the last one.

Do you work with medical offices, doctors, and dentists? +

Yes — we embroider polos, scrubs, jackets, and lab coats for medical, dental, chiropractic, and veterinary offices around Onalaska and Livingston. Add individual staff names for $3 per piece.

Can you handle sports teams and school groups? +

Absolutely. Team uniforms with player names and numbers, coach polos, booster-club fan gear, warmups. Order 3-4 weeks before your season for standard timing.

Do you offer net-30 invoicing for businesses? +

For established business accounts with recurring orders, yes. Reach out at hello@lakesidethreadz.com and we'll set up terms.
