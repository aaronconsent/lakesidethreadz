Why this hat is our most-asked-for

The Richardson 112 is the trucker hat you've seen a thousand times without knowing the name. Cotton-poly front that takes an embroidered logo cleanly, plastic mesh back that keeps your head cool in a Texas summer, curved brim, snapback closure — it's the shape and fit most people picture when they say "trucker hat."

We stock it as the everyday go-to for business crews, sports teams, church staff, chamber events, family reunions, and lake weekends. A polo doesn't fit everyone, but this hat does.

What we decorate it with

Embroidered logos — the classic. Left front, ~2-3 inches wide, stitched directly onto the foam panel. Turnaround: 5–10 business days. Simple logos $15–20, more detailed $20–30, 3D puff embroidery (raised look) $25–35.

DTF transfer — full-color prints on the front panel when you need photo-quality artwork the embroidery can't reproduce. Same panel, different technique.

Sleeve/side detail — small logo or text on the side panel or brim. Nice for adding a phone number or website without cluttering the front.

Frequently asked

How much does one really cost?

The blank hat is one part of the price; the decoration is the other. For a small logo left-front, expect the finished piece to land in a common range — Kristen will send an exact number based on your artwork and quantity. Bigger orders (12+, 24+) unlock better per-piece pricing.

Can I get 3D puff embroidery?

Yes. Puff adds a raised, dimensional look to bolder shapes — think block letters, mascots, simple icons. Not every logo works for puff (very thin lines or gradients don't translate). Kristen will flag it if your design won't puff cleanly and suggest a flat alternative.

How long does an order take?

Typical turnaround is 5–10 business days from proof approval. Rush turnaround is available for time-sensitive events — mention your deadline in the quote and Kristen will let you know what's possible.

What if I want a different color?

Richardson 112 comes in a wide color range — solid crowns, two-tone (color front, black or grey back), and camo variants. Kristen will confirm color options when she writes your quote.

Do you have a lower-profile version?

Yes — the Richardson 115 is the low-profile version. Shorter crown, same shape otherwise. Popular for women's fit and boutique looks. Ask about it in the quote and Kristen will send both options.
